## What it is

I designed and built an automated pan-and-tilt servo platform that mounts thermal cameras on a vehicle roof for manual or automated wildlife observation.

## How it’s built

- **Firmware:** ESP32, written in C with the Arduino IDE and GitHub Copilot.
- **Controller:** an Android application.
- **Modes:** manual movement, and automatic left-to-right scanning.
- **UX:** designed with Google Sketch.

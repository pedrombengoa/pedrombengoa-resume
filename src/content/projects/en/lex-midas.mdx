## What it is

Lex Midas is a production-oriented application I designed and built for lawyers in Argentina. It uses AI to help them draft legal documents, and it manages the lifecycle of their legal cases.

## How it’s built

- **Next.js and React** for the application, styled with **Tailwind CSS**.
- **Supabase** as the backend platform.
- **Vercel AI SDK** for the AI features.
- **Resend** for email.
- **Mercado Pago** for payments.
- Deployed on **Vercel**.

## What it is

I’m collaborating with a team of 15 veterinarians who serve about 50 dairy farms. Together we’re building a web and native mobile platform for standardized herd-health reporting, business intelligence, and action planning.

## How it’s built

- **Web application:** React and Vite.
- **Native application:** React Native and Expo, with **WatermelonDB** for offline-capable workflows.
- **Distribution:** Expo for generating the Android APK.
- **Process:** built with AgentOS and Spec-driven Development practices.

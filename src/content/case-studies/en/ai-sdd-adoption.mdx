## The problem

In early June 2026, the company required every development team to complete an AI course based on Spec-driven Development by the end of the month. The three teams I work with already had full backlogs. Only two of them used AI, each with its own ad hoc agents, skills, and procedures. None of them used the newly introduced MCPs for Bitbucket, Jira, Confluence, MongoDB, SQL Server, Splunk, OpenShift, and other systems.

## My role

Help all three teams meet the deadline, and make the result a consistent, useful engineering practice instead of a compliance checkbox.

## What I did

- Completed the mandatory training first, so I could translate it into practical guidance.
- Demonstrated what the MCPs could do to each Solution Lead.
- Coached team members and leaders through the material.
- Consolidated the teams’ fragmented approaches into one company-approved AI and SDD workflow.

## Result

All three teams met the deadline and converged on a standardized approach that uses the company’s MCP tooling. I became a go-to resource for AI and SDD adoption across the teams.

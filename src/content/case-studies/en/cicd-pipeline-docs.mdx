## The problem

When I joined ITX, my team was among the first to move into full-stack work. The client had backend CI/CD pipelines, but nobody inside ITX understood them well, and there was no documentation.

## My role

Make onboarding a backend service repeatable for my team, and eventually for the wider organization.

## What I did

- Reverse-engineered the pipeline and documented it.
- Trained my team on it.
- Gave a formal presentation to the wider organization, with slides and a walkthrough.

## Result

An undocumented, poorly understood process became a reusable reference. My team could reliably onboard backend services, and the knowledge spread to other teams.

## The problem

The company decided to migrate a large portfolio of Java microservices from OpenShift 3 to OpenShift 4. It was more than a version bump. Teams also had to adapt to changes in how routes and Application Load Balancers (ALBs) worked.

## My role

As Solution Lead, learn the new routing and ALB model, set a practical migration approach, train the team, and coordinate the migration without disrupting delivery.

## What I did

- Learned the OpenShift 3-to-4 upgrade process and the related route and ALB changes.
- Turned that knowledge into guidance for the team and trained the other developers.
- Applied the approach across the portfolio together with the team, troubleshooting migration issues and coordinating the work across about 25 Java microservices.

## Result

The team migrated about 25 applications from OpenShift 3 to OpenShift 4 in three months. Along the way, the knowledge spread across the team, so the developers could do the migration work together instead of depending on one person.

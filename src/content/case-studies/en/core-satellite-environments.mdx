## The problem

The company was introducing decentralized, isolated testing environments based on IaaS concepts such as Terraform. Adopting them changed how teams branched, tested, and integrated their work. The two teams chosen to go first worked in Payments, on a payment rail that handles several million dollars a day.

## My role

Lead the company-wide adoption, and make the two payment teams early adopters without putting reliability at risk.

## What I did

- Drove the adoption effort across the organization.
- Guided both teams to work in dedicated environments, independent of the main branch.
- Managed the integration and testing of about three months of accumulated work.

## Result

Both teams worked independently while integrating and testing the accumulated changes. That validated the model in a high-risk domain and gave the broader rollout an early success story.
